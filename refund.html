Refund policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

We want RINN to work for you. If it doesn't, you can get your money back.

14-day refund

Within 14 days of buying, you can ask for a full refund if RINN won't run on your computer and we can't fix it together. Send us what you see (a screenshot of the Setup page helps) and we'll try to sort it out first.

How to ask

Reply to your purchase receipt email, or use the contact option on the store page where you bought RINN. Include your order number.

When refunds don't apply

  • More than 14 days after purchase.
  • Your computer doesn't meet the requirements listed on the website (Windows 10 or 11, about 16 GB of RAM recommended).
  • Your YouTube project hasn't passed YouTube's API audit yet. This is YouTube's process, and RINN's Assisted mode works in the meantime.
  • Your channel's views, income or growth. RINN does the work of editing and posting, but results depend on your content.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the payment method you used, through the store that handled your purchase.
