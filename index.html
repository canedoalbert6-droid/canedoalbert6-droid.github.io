How it works

From raw clip to scheduled Short

Each clip moves down the line by itself. You only step in where you want to: approving an edit or tweaking a title.

  1. DropDrag videos onto the office or into the inbox folder.
  2. Edit9:16 crop, silence cuts, word-by-word captions, hook line.
  3. RevisePreview it. Type "captions bigger" or "cut first 2 sec".
  4. LoadChecks 1080×1920, ≤60 s, saves the final MP4.
  5. CaptionTitle under 60 characters, description, 3–5 hashtags, tags.
  6. ScheduleBooks the next free slot in your time zone.
  7. PostUploads to YouTube with the publish time set.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I have to pay for AI or any API?

No. Titles, hooks and captions are written by a local model through Ollama, and speech is transcribed by Whisper on your PC. An optional Gemini free-tier backup exists, but it's off unless you turn it on.

Will my Shorts go public automatically?

Yes, once your Google project passes YouTube's free API audit. Until then YouTube keeps API uploads private, so RINN switches to Assisted mode: a ready-to-paste card with the file, title and hashtags for each slot.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. RINN is built and tested on Windows 10 and 11.

Will this make my channel successful?

RINN handles the busywork and learns from your own results, but views depend on your content. Nobody can promise views or income, and we don't.

Can I use clips from other creators?

Only post videos and music you have the rights to. Re-uploading other people's footage can bring copyright claims or a "reused content" decision that blocks monetization. Your own voice-over, hook text and edits make a Short yours, and the Bot Manager warns you about clips that have none.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Only to YouTube, when a Short is uploaded. Videos, transcripts, captions and your sign-in stay on your PC, with a daily backup in a local folder.
